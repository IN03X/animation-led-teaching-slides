Times New Roman, nonitalic. Title33, subtitle31 bold, body generally25-29, result31-35, main route42 in the original engine's logical font units. These logical units should be compared in rendered output, not assumed identical to a different tool's point system. Math generated with standalone LaTeX, amsmath/upgreek/xcolor, upright bold roman plus upright Greek; glyph-outline SVG embedded with high-resolution PNG fallback. Preserve aspect ratio and baseline consistency. Actual formula size should generally stay above20 logical units, not shrink long fractions into unreadability.

Diagrams typically logical1120 x250, raster2240 x500. Position is adjusted to the particular page; there is no universal fixed formula Y coordinate. The golden looks consistent because its relationships are consistent, not because every formula occupies an identical band.
